This page asks for your location as soon as it opens, and only if you allow it. Coordinates are sent every 5 seconds while this page stays open, and stop when you close it or revoke permission. Those same coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo and BigDataCloud to look up real weather and place names. Until a real reading arrives, the card above shows labelled demonstration values that are not your local conditions.
